**"The 21% is a defect rate to be driven down."**

**"A deterministic failure is a real content gap to fix."**
Determinism proves consistency, not correctness. A rubric item that fails every single time might be a real gap in SKILL.md — or a rubric demanding something the skill was deliberately designed not to do. Repeatability tells you *which* number, not *whose* fault. You cannot read the fix off the determinism alone.

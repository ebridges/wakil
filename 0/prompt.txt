The linked files should be passed through as related files, but with a higher priority than those found by `search_workspace()` since they are files that are distinctly identified by the user.  Confirm whether that would address the gap you point out.

Specifically here are the three issues:

---

We're bloating the context with too much irrelevant information.

The `search_workspace()` function returns a **lot** of files, most of which are irrelevant.  This is due to the fact that we're passing the entire contents of the `@` referenced files in the query.

When ingesting a transcript, it should create a relevant title, and an abstract of about 300 chars, both saved in the sources/transcript note frontmatter.

Then, the title and the abstract should be used in enrich for QMD search.


Related: the `candidates` regexp search in `_candidate_entity_notes` in `ingest_service.py` ends up including a lot of irrelevant terms -- e.g. small talk, speech terms (e.g. Mm-hmm, Very, etc.): is there a way to filter those out? Because candidates ends up with a lot of noise, "matches" ends up having a lot of irrelevant stuff and that function's returned collection bloats the context.

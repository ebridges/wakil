Compiled Truth + Timeline — for a note about an accumulating subject: one
that gets touched by multiple, separate sources over time (an identity, or
any other type tracked and updated across its life), not a single dated
occurrence. The two sections have opposite update disciplines and must not
be confused:

- **Compiled Truth** is *re-synthesized* on every update to cover the union
  of what was already known plus whatever's new — never replaced with only
  the newest source's content. This is where texture goes: what the subject
  believes, what it's building, what's driving it — not a bare fact sheet.
- **Timeline / Log** is *append-only, reverse-chronological*. New dated
  entries get added; existing entries — including auto-generated back-link
  lines — are never deleted, rewritten, or reordered.

Skeleton:

```markdown
# {Display Name}

## Compiled Truth

{synthesized, always-current summary — cite non-obvious claims inline}
- {state fields relevant to this subject}

### Open threads
- {what's unresolved}

Cross-references: [[other-entity]]

---

## Timeline / Log

### {date} — source: {kind}
- {what happened / what was learned, cited}
```
